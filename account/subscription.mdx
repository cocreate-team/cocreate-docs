Cocreate offers two subscription tiers designed to fit both working professionals and users who want to explore the app before committing. The **Pro** plan unlocks unlimited analysis and every feature in the app for a flat monthly fee. The **Permafree** plan is free forever and gives you full access to the interface, playback, and export tools — with a small cap on the AI analysis pipeline. Both plans include [airgapped mode](/account/airgapped-mode) for offline and secure-set workflows.

## What Are Analysis Minutes?

Analysis minutes are the unit Cocreate uses to meter AI processing. One analysis minute equals one minute of media analyzed — meaning if you run the full analysis pipeline (slate detection, transcription, metadata extraction) on a 10-minute clip, that consumes 10 analysis minutes.

Analysis runs when you press **Analyze** on imported media with slate reading or transcription enabled. Review, playback, export, and Card Manager transfers do not consume analysis minutes.

To check your current usage, open **Settings → Account**. Your remaining minutes and your plan's limit (or "Unlimited" for Pro) are shown in the usage summary.

## Starting a Pro Trial

The 14-day Pro trial gives you full access to unlimited analysis from the moment you sign up — no credit card required at the start.

1. Open Cocreate and sign in or create an account.
2. In **Settings → Account**, press **Start free trial**.
3. Your trial begins immediately. Cocreate displays the number of trial days remaining in the account panel.

At the end of the 14-day trial, if you have not added a payment method, your account automatically converts to Permafree. No charge is made and no data is deleted — your projects remain intact, but analysis is capped at 2 minutes per project going forward.

## Managing Your Subscription

Cocreate uses Stripe for all billing. To update your payment method, change your plan, or view invoices:

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Press **Manage subscription**.
3. The Stripe customer portal opens in your browser. From there you can update payment details, view billing history, and cancel or reactivate your subscription.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Your account remains on Pro until that date, then converts to Permafree.

## Discount Codes, Activation Codes, and Referral Credits

Cocreate supports several ways to reduce or waive your subscription cost:

- **Discount codes** — apply a percentage or fixed discount to your monthly charge. Enter the code in the Stripe checkout when you subscribe, or in **Settings → Account → Redeem code**.
- **Activation codes** — admin-issued codes that grant a complimentary Pro license (used for beta testers, partner licenses, and production comps). Enter an activation code in **Settings → Account → Redeem code**. Activation codes do not require a payment method.
- **Referral credits** — when someone signs up using your referral link, both you and the new user receive subscription credits applied automatically to future billing cycles. Find your referral link in **Settings → Account → Referrals**.

## What Happens When the Permafree Limit Is Reached

On the Permafree plan, analysis pauses automatically once a project reaches the 2-analysis-minute cap. The rest of the app continues to work normally — you can still play back media, edit metadata manually, export to your NLE, and publish to the dashboard. Only AI-powered processing (slate reading, transcription, and the corrections pipeline) stops until you upgrade.

When analysis pauses, Cocreate displays a prompt in the processing panel offering to upgrade to Pro. Press **Upgrade to Pro** to open the subscription flow and enter a payment method. Once your Pro subscription activates, analysis resumes from where it left off — you do not need to re-import or reprocess the project from scratch.

## Upgrading from Permafree to Pro Mid-Project

You can upgrade at any point during a project without losing any existing work. Metadata you entered manually, sync pairs already computed, and any partial transcripts remain in place. After upgrading, run **Analyze** again on the assets that were paused to complete their analysis. Cocreate picks up only the unprocessed portions, so you are not charged for work already done.
