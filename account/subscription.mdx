Cocreate offers two subscription tiers designed to fit both working professionals and users who want to explore the app before committing. The **Pro** plan unlocks unlimited analysis and every feature in the app for a flat monthly fee. The **Permafree** plan is free forever and gives you full access to the interface, playback, and export tools, with a small cap on the AI analysis pipeline. Both plans include [airgapped mode](/account/airgapped-mode) for offline and secure-set workflows.

## Plan comparison

| | **Permafree** | **Pro** |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free, forever | ~$99 / month |
| **Free trial** | — | 7 days, no credit card required |
| **Analysis minutes** | 2 minutes per month | Unlimited |
| **Slate detection** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Transcription** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **NLE export** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Dashboard publishing** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Airgapped mode** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Reports and receipts** | ✓ | ✓ |

## What are analysis minutes?

Analysis minutes are the unit Cocreate uses to meter AI processing. One analysis minute equals one minute of media analyzed. If you run the full analysis pipeline (slate detection, transcription, metadata extraction) on a 10-minute clip, that consumes 10 analysis minutes.

Analysis runs when you press **Process** on imported media with slate reading or transcription enabled. Review, playback, export, and Card Manager transfers do not consume analysis minutes.

To check your current usage, open **Account & Billing** from the sidebar. Your remaining minutes and your plan's limit (or "Unlimited" for Pro) are shown in the usage summary.

## Starting a Pro trial

The 7-day Pro trial gives you full access to unlimited analysis from the moment you sign up. No credit card is required at the start.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Open Cocreate and create an account or sign in for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trial starts automatically">
    Cocreate starts your 7-day Pro trial as soon as your account is created. There is no separate button to press.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track days remaining">
    The trial countdown appears in the app tab bar and in **Account & Billing**. See [Trial and quota visibility](#trial-and-quota-visibility) below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

At the end of the 7-day trial, if you have not added a payment method, your account converts to Permafree automatically. Cocreate makes no charge and deletes no data. Your projects remain intact, and analysis is capped at 2 minutes per month going forward.

## Trial and quota visibility

Cocreate keeps your trial or free-quota status visible from the moment you open the app, so you never have to open **Account & Billing** just to check where you stand.

### Countdown chip in the tab bar

While you are on a trial or on Permafree, a small countdown chip sits in the app tab bar next to the build info:

- **On a trial:** the chip reads "N days left in trial". It stays blue while there is time on the clock, turns amber when 3 days or fewer remain, and turns red on the last day ("Trial ends today").
- **On Permafree:** the chip reads "N.N min left", counting down your remaining analysis minutes for the current month. It stays neutral while you have quota and turns red once you drop below half a minute.

The chip is informational, not clickable. To act on it, open **Account & Billing** from the sidebar.

Paid Pro accounts, accounts with unlimited-access bypass, and accounts whose billing is past due do not see the chip.

### Plan badge

Every place Cocreate shows your plan uses the same badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pro** | Paid Pro subscription, active. |
| **Pro Trial** or **Trial** | Trial in progress. Says "Pro Trial" once a Stripe subscription is attached, otherwise "Trial". |
| **Canceling** | Pro subscription that will end at the current period boundary. |
| **Free** | Permafree. |
| **Unlimited** | Complimentary access granted by Cocreate staff. |
| **Past due** | Payment failed on the most recent invoice. |

The badge on the **Account & Billing** screen and the shortened chip in the tab bar always match.

### Daily trial reminder

Once a day, on app open, Cocreate shows a short reminder dialog for accounts still on trial. The dialog shows how many days remain, the exact date the trial ends, and an **Upgrade to Pro** button that opens the Stripe checkout. Dismissing the dialog silences it until the next calendar day, on the machine where you dismissed it.

The reminder waits for other startup dialogs (like beta warnings or the tutorial welcome) to close first, and does not show for Permafree, paid, past-due, or unlimited-access accounts.

## Managing your subscription

Cocreate uses Stripe for all billing. To update your payment method, change your plan, or view invoices:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account & Billing">
    Select **Account & Billing** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Manage Subscription">
    The Stripe customer portal opens in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make changes in Stripe">
    From the portal you can update payment details, view billing history, and cancel or reactivate your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Your account remains on Pro until that date, then converts to Permafree. The plan badge switches to **Canceling** for the remainder of the period.

## Discount codes, activation codes, and referral credits

Cocreate supports several ways to reduce or waive your subscription cost:

- **Discount codes.** Apply a percentage or fixed discount to your monthly charge. Enter the code in the Stripe checkout when you subscribe, or in **Account & Billing → Redeem code**.
- **Activation codes.** Admin-issued codes that grant a complimentary Pro license, used for beta testers, partner licenses, and production comps. Enter an activation code in **Account & Billing → Redeem code**. Activation codes do not require a payment method.
- **Referral credits.** When someone signs up using your referral link, both you and the new user receive subscription credits applied automatically to future billing cycles. Find your referral link in **Account & Billing → Referrals**.

## What happens when the Permafree limit is reached

On the Permafree plan, analysis pauses automatically once your account reaches the 2-analysis-minute monthly cap. The rest of the app continues to work normally. You can still play back media, edit metadata manually, export to your NLE, and publish to the dashboard. Only AI-powered processing (slate reading, transcription, and the corrections pipeline) stops until your quota resets or you upgrade.

When analysis pauses, Cocreate displays a prompt offering to upgrade to Pro. Press **Upgrade to Pro** to open the subscription flow and enter a payment method. Once your Pro subscription activates, analysis resumes from where it left off. You do not need to re-import or reprocess the project from scratch.

## Upgrading from Permafree to Pro mid-project

You can upgrade at any point during a project without losing any existing work. Metadata you entered manually, sync pairs already computed, and any partial transcripts remain in place. After upgrading, run **Process** again on the assets that were paused to complete their analysis. Cocreate picks up only the unprocessed portions, so you are not charged for work already done.
