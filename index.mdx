Cocreate is a professional desktop application for film and TV post-production, built to automate every stage of the dailies pipeline — from checksum-verified camera card offload through AI-powered slate detection, audio/video sync, and native NLE export. Whether you work as an assistant editor, DIT operator, data wrangler, or at a post facility, Cocreate replaces the patchwork of manual tools and scripts that slow down your day-one handoff.
