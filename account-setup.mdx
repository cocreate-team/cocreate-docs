Before you can analyze media or export projects, you need a Cocreate account. Accounts are free to create, and every new account starts with a 14-day Pro trial so you can evaluate the full feature set before choosing a plan. This page covers creating an account, understanding the two plans, and configuring your operator profile — the initials and metadata that appear on transfer receipts, ingest logs, and production paperwork.

## Creating an account

Cocreate supports two sign-in methods. Both create the same type of account; the choice is a matter of preference.

### Email and password

1. Open Cocreate and click **Create account** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
3. Check your inbox for a verification email from Cocreate and click the confirmation link.
4. Return to Cocreate — you are signed in and your 14-day Pro trial begins automatically.

To reset a forgotten password, click **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. Cocreate sends a reset link to your registered email address.

### Google OAuth

1. Open Cocreate and click **Continue with Google**.
2. Cocreate opens the Google authorization page in your **system browser** (not an in-app webview).
3. Sign in to your Google account and grant the requested permissions.
4. Your browser redirects through the `cocreate://` deep link back to Cocreate, completing the sign-in automatically.

No password is set or stored on your Cocreate account when you sign in with Google. If you later need to access Cocreate without Google, use the **Forgot password** flow to set a standalone password.

**Analysis minutes** are metered by the total duration of media you send through the AI analysis pipeline (slate detection + transcription) in a single project. Importing and organizing media, editing metadata manually, and exporting NLE projects do not consume analysis minutes.

When your Pro trial ends, your account converts to Permafree automatically — you keep all your projects and exported files, and the 2-minute limit applies to new analysis runs from that point forward.

To subscribe or manage your billing, open **Settings → Subscription** inside Cocreate, or visit the client dashboard at [dashboard.cocreate.so](https://dashboard.cocreate.so). Billing is handled through Stripe; Cocreate never stores your payment card details.

## Setting your operator profile

Your operator initials appear on transfer receipts, MHL manifests, ingest log entries, and any production paperwork Cocreate generates. Set them before you run your first transfer.

1. Open **Settings** from the Cocreate menu bar (macOS: **Cocreate → Settings**; Windows/Linux: the gear icon in the navigation).
2. Go to the **General** tab.
3. Enter your **Operator initials** — typically two or three uppercase letters (for example, `AE` or `JRS`).
4. Click **Save**.

You can update your operator initials at any time. Changes apply to all new transfers and log entries from that point; they do not retroactively update existing receipts.

## Signing out and switching accounts

To sign out of Cocreate:

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Click **Sign out**.

Cocreate clears your session and returns to the sign-in screen. Your project files (`.ccrt`) remain on disk and are not deleted when you sign out. When you sign back in — or sign in with a different account — you can reopen projects from the Home screen normally.

<Note>
  If you share a machine with another operator, each person should sign in with their own account so that receipts and ingest log entries carry the correct operator attribution.
</Note>

## Airgapped mode

If you work on an air-gapped network or a set with no internet access, Cocreate can run entirely offline. In airgapped mode:

- Your session is restored from an encrypted local cache — no network authentication required.
- Cloud AI services (Deepgram transcription, GPT-4o slate reads) are disabled.
- Slate detection falls back to the bundled YOLO model only.
- On macOS, local transcription uses the bundled Parakeet CoreML model on the Apple Neural Engine.
- Usage is buffered locally and reconciled with the server when connectivity is restored.
- Billing and subscription UI are hidden.

No account or credentials are required to use airgapped mode — the cached session is sufficient. For full setup instructions, see [Airgapped Mode](/account/airgapped-mode).
